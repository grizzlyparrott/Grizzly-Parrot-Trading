Methodology, limits, and citation

What is normalized

The atlas preserves each market's native displayed quote convention. Grain and livestock increments are shown in U.S. cents because that is how those futures are quoted; Treasury fractions are also shown as decimal point equivalents. The dollar-per-tick value is the common comparison field.

What is not included

Broker day margins, exchange performance bonds, live prices, options ticks, calendar-spread ticks, fees, and slippage are not included. Those values either change frequently or use different rules. A null or omitted field is never replaced with an estimate.

Verification policy

Every row requires an official exchange source, internal tick-math reconciliation, and a recorded verification date. Corrections can be sent to grizzlyparrott04@gmail.com.

Use or cite this dataset

You may quote individual rows, use the CSV/JSON in educational work, or link readers to this reference. Attribute the source so readers can verify the methodology and update date.
