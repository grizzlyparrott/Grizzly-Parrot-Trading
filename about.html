Research focus

What Kyle researches

The work focuses on how financial markets function, how their underlying mechanics shape price and execution, and how those relationships change over time.

  • 01

    Futures and contract mechanics

    Contract specifications, tick value, expiration, settlement, margin, and liquidity across delivery months.

  • 02

    Currencies and central-bank policy

    Foreign-exchange futures, rate differentials, policy transmission, intervention, and the links between cash and futures markets.

  • 03

    Market structure, liquidity, and execution

    Order books, spreads, slippage, market impact, and the difference between a displayed price and an executable result.

  • 04

    Cross-market and historical research

    Relationships among yields, currencies, metals, energy, and equities across different events and market regimes.

Research approach. Work published by Grizzly Parrot Trading generally starts with primary or authoritative records where available, including government and central-bank records, exchange documentation, regulatory material, historical datasets, and market data. Calculations, assumptions, sample definitions, and limitations are stated where they materially affect the result.
