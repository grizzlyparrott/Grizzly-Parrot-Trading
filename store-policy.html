Print Book Store Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

Price and Availability

Each paperback is $39.00 USD and each hardcover is $49.00 USD. Print books are made on demand and are currently available only for delivery to addresses in the United States.

Shipping

Shipping is charged separately. The available services and exact cost are calculated from the delivery address and displayed before payment. Applicable tax, if any, is also displayed before payment. Delivery estimates are estimates and may be affected by print production, carrier operations, weather, or other circumstances outside our control.

Cancellations

To request a cancellation, email grizzlyparrott04@gmail.com as soon as possible and include the order reference. Cancellation is possible only before the printer begins production and cannot be guaranteed.

Returns and Exchanges

Because each paperback and hardcover is printed on demand, we do not accept returns or exchanges for a change of mind, an accidental order, or a preference concerning the book's content or format.

Damaged, Defective, Incorrect, or Missing Books

If a book arrives damaged or defective, is packed incorrectly, or is missing, contact grizzlyparrott04@gmail.com within 30 days of shipment. Include the order reference and a description of the issue. Photos of the book and packaging may be requested when relevant. Eligible claims will be resolved with a replacement at no additional charge.

Questions

For order support, email grizzlyparrott04@gmail.com. See the Privacy Policy for information about order data.
