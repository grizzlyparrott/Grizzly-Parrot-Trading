The central problem

Diversification disappears when the driver is shared.

A portfolio can contain five currency pairs and still express one risk. Funding conditions, dollar liquidity, commodity demand, or risk sentiment can run through every position at the same time.

When the regime changes, correlations tighten and the independence you thought you owned disappears. This book explains the mechanism before it becomes a drawdown.
