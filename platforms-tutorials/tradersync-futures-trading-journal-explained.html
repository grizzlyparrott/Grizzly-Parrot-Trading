Futures-specific value

The Features That Matter Most

Journal + analytics

Turn raw fills into reviewable evidence

Contract-level reports, trade charts, tags, granular breakdowns, MFE/MAE, fees, and filters make it faster to compare results by setup, time, account, direction, and outcome.

Market Replay

Revisit price action instead of relying on memory

Futures replay ranges from one-minute to 250ms precision by plan. Official support says history starts in September 2024 and Replay appears the following day at about 7:00 a.m.

Cypher AI

Ask better questions of your own journal

Cypher looks for patterns and plan adherence. Its value depends on complete imports and consistent tags; treat the output as a lead to investigate, not a trading signal.

Fit matters more than features

Who Should Use It—and Who Should Not

Strongest fit

Active futures traders who review consistently, manage several accounts, tag setups, and want analytics plus replay in one service.

Weakest fit

Low-frequency traders, anyone unwilling to reconcile imports, or anyone expecting signals, brokerage execution, or guaranteed improvement.

Make the seven days count

A Focused Trial Checklist

  1. Import the exact broker, platform, and account types you use.
  2. Match contracts, timestamps, fees, fills, and net P&L to the broker statement.
  3. Test micros, minis, scale-outs, rollovers, and multiple accounts.
  4. Tag twenty trades using labels you would keep.
  5. Run one useful report, replay session, and Cypher question.
  6. Verify cancellation before choosing a paid plan.

What to verify during the trial

Official pages conflict on autosync versus file imports, some account limits, and whether mobile access requires Elite. Confirm those items in your account before paying annually.

Also confirm that post-September-2024 futures history and your plan's replay precision fit your trading style.

Final verdict

TraderSync Has a Real Job to Do

TraderSync appears capable of turning scattered fills and notes into a repeatable review process. If it imports your records cleanly and saves enough time to justify the subscription, it is worth using. The free trial is the correct way to find out.
