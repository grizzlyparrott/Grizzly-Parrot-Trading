About the library

What This Site Is—and What It Is Not

Grizzly Parrot Trading is an educational reference site. Its job is to make futures mechanics, market structure, risk, software workflows, and account rules easier to inspect. It is not a brokerage, signal service, personalized advisory service, or promise of trading results. Detailed guides cite first-party exchange, regulator, platform, or rulebook sources where specifications and rules matter.

Educational and risk disclaimer

Grizzly Parrot Trading provides general education, not individualized financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Futures are leveraged, complex, and can produce substantial losses. Examples, diagrams, and calculators are simplified planning aids—not live market data, predictions, recommendations, or fill guarantees. Verify current exchange specifications, broker rules, and your own financial capacity before trading; use only risk capital you can afford to lose.
